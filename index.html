SprintTime

Sprint Timing & Video Analysis

Measure sprint times from recorded videos with frame-by-frame analysis. SprintTime helps athletes and coaches analyze running performance, identify start and finish moments, measure reaction time, and create timed performance videos.

  • 100m and 200m sprint timing
  • Frame-by-frame video navigation
  • Reaction time measurement
  • Multiple finish points and timed video export

DynamicCrop

Dynamic Video Cropping & Reframing

Crop and reframe videos dynamically on Android. DynamicCrop lets you zoom, pan, rotate, and adjust the visible area throughout a video using manual keyframes and automatic keyframe generation where supported.

  • Dynamic video cropping and reframing
  • Zoom and pan controls
  • Manual and automatic keyframes
  • Preview and export edited videos
